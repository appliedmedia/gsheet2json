# Welcome to gsheet2json Discussions

gsheet2json round-trips a Google Sheet to clean, structured JSON and back, preserving formatting, validations, formulas, and font colors. This is the community space for it. Pull up a chair.

## Where things go

* **Have a question**: post in **Q&A**. How do I export a tab, why did an import skip a style, what does a scope prompt mean. No question is too small.
* **Have an idea**: post in **Ideas**. Feature requests, JSON-schema suggestions, workflow wishes. Upvotes help us prioritize.
* **Built something cool**: post in **Show and tell**. AI pipelines that edit a tab as JSON and re-import it, dashboards, automations. We love seeing these.
* **Found a bug or need support**: please file it at [g2j.support](<https://g2j.support>) instead of here, so it gets tracked and triaged.

## Before you post

* Skim existing discussions and issues first; your question may already be answered.
* For anything reproducible, describe the shape of your sheet, what you did, what happened, and what you expected.
* Keep it kind and on-topic. We follow a be-excellent-to-each-other standard.

## A note on privacy

gsheet2json runs inside your own Google account and your sheet data never leaves it. This forum, though, is public. When you paste JSON or screenshots to illustrate a question, scrub anything private (names, emails, financials) first.
